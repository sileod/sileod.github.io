I am a tenured researcher at Inria Lille working on natural language processing and language models.

My research focuses on reasoning, evaluation and data. I study how language models acquire and apply symbolic reasoning capabilities, how these capabilities can be evaluated reliably, and how procedural environments and synthetic data can support model training. I also work on datasets, benchmarks, data provenance and open research infrastructure.

## Current research

### Reasoning and training environments

Procedural generation, formal logic, symbolic reasoning, synthetic data and training environments for language models.

### Evaluation and benchmarks

Long-context reasoning, attention limitations, expert-level evaluation and the construction of challenging, auditable benchmarks.

### Data and research infrastructure

Dataset collections, task standardization, data provenance, licensing and reusable open-source tools for NLP research.

## Selected work

- [Reasoning Core](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02208): a scalable procedural data generation suite for symbolic pre-training, post-training, evaluation and reinforcement learning. [Software](https://github.com/sileod/reasoning-core)
- [Humanity's Last Exam](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09962-4): an expert-level benchmark for assessing advanced AI capabilities, published in *Nature*.
- [Logic Haystacks](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-short.3/): a long-context evaluation of logical reasoning without easily identifiable unrelated padding.
- [Bridging the Data Provenance Gap Across Text, Speech, and Video](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G5DziesYxL): a multimodal extension of the Data Provenance Initiative, published at ICLR 2025.
- [Scaling Synthetic Logical Reasoning Datasets with Context-Sensitive Declarative Grammars](https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-main.301/): the paper introducing the approach implemented in [gramforge](https://github.com/sileod/gramforge).
- [tasksource](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.1361/): a structured collection of hundreds of interoperable NLP tasks. [Software](https://github.com/sileod/tasksource)
